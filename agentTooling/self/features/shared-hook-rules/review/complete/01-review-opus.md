# 01 — review: shared-hook-rules

Review the `shared-hook-rules` build against the spec below — the URGENT item that headed
`self/BACKLOG.md` on `origin/main` ("the hook's helpers duplicate `analysis/`'s rules instead
of sharing them") and the coordinator rulings 1–10 in
`self/features/shared-hook-rules/NOTES.md`, written before the build. Judge from those and the
diff, never from the implementer's report. "No findings" is a legitimate verdict.

## What the feature was supposed to do

A pure refactor with one structural outcome: every rule the PreToolUse hook shares with
`analysis/` (and that a top-level `*.sh` entry point restates) has ONE definition, in the
stdlib-only module `hooks/shared_rules.py`, which the hook, `analysis/` and the shell all read.

- The orchestration record roots (`self/orchestrations`, `plans/orchestrations`), the record
  name, the `.git` entry, the fence keys `kind`/`session`/`features` and the orchestrator
  feature keys, the `coordinator` kind, the ledger name, the slug pattern, and the two refusal
  texts `orchestration-start.sh` restated — each spelled once, in `shared_rules.py`.
- `analysis/roots.py` (and `manifest.py`, `routing.py`) and `hooks/orchestrator_role.py` bind
  their existing names to those values; their callers are unchanged.
- `orchestration-start.sh` reads the values from the module rather than restating them, and
  refuses (never falls back to a literal) when it cannot.
- `hooks/wire-settings.py` builds its record ask rules from the constants; the generated
  settings are byte-identical.
- The mirror tests (U3 in `self/tests/orchestrator-role.sh`, R10's equality checks in
  `self/tests/orchestration-records-in-repo.sh`) are gone, replaced by
  `self/tests/shared-rules.sh` asserting the single definition and the fail-safe paths.
- The backlog item is removed; anything deliberately left (the shell slug patterns at least)
  has a backlog entry.

Excluded: any behaviour change. Every refusal text, exit code, hook answer, record location
and generated byte is what it was on `origin/main`. `brief_header.py` and `env_profile.py`
were already single and need not move.

## Contracts to hold it to

1. **Single definition.** No constant in `hooks/*.py` or `hooks/allow-repo-commands.sh`
   restates (same meaning, same value) one in `analysis/*.py` or a top-level `*.sh`; check
   by reading, not only by the new test — a renamed copy is still a copy. Each remaining
   apparent pair is justified in NOTES.md (rulings 9, 10 and the build rulings).
2. **`grep -rn 'MIRRORS\|MIRRORED' hooks/ analysis/ *.sh`** is empty, or every hit is
   justified in NOTES.md.
3. **The hook stays fast and fails safe** (ruling 4, 5): `shared_rules.py` imports only the
   stdlib, does no I/O or subprocess at import; the hook adds no `sys.path` entry outside its
   own directory and no process per call; a `hooks/` lacking `shared_rules.py` degrades the
   role rule exactly as one lacking `orchestrator_role.py` does and never crashes a Bash call.
   `analysis/` appends (never inserts) the hooks directory to `sys.path`.
4. **The shell fails closed**: `orchestration-start.sh` refuses, naming the module, when the
   values cannot be read; there is no `eval` of module output.
5. **Behaviour unchanged**: the refusal texts users see (STANDALONE, LEGACY), the record
   paths written and read under `--self` and without it, the ledger written, the role
   decisions of `hooks/orchestrator_role.py` (including the coordinator exemption by shape and
   all-fences reading), the generated `.claude/settings.json` bytes. Every pre-existing test
   in `self/tests/` passes with no assertion weakened — only the equality-of-copies checks
   U3 and R10 are removed, and the test that replaces them asserts at least as much.
6. **The new test** `self/tests/shared-rules.sh` is registered in `self/gate.sh` (both the
   list and a `record` line) and has its row in `self/tests/README.md`; fixtures that copy
   `orchestrator_role.py` alone now copy `shared_rules.py` too.
7. **READMEs** (`CONVENTIONS.md` → "Keeping READMEs up to date"): `hooks/README.md` has an
   entry for `shared_rules.py` naming who reads it and how it fails; `analysis/README.md`'s
   MIRRORED entries are rewritten; `self/PROJECT_FACTS.md` names the module where it names
   `brief_header.py`; `check-readme-index.py` passes.
8. **Named constants** (`CONVENTIONS.md` → "Named constants") in every file touched; the
   CLI's usage/exit codes are constants.
